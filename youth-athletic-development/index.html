What The Classes Will Focus On

Athletic Foundation

Building better movement quality, coordination, balance, body control, and confidence.

Speed & Quickness

Training acceleration, change of direction, footwork, reaction, and sharper movement.

Agility & Balance

Helping athletes move with control, stay stable, and handle sport-specific movement better.

Explosive Power

Developing jump ability, force production, and athletic power through age-appropriate training.
