Training sessions are paid monthly in full, just like your membership fee. This is the easiest way to track payments and ensure accountability for both of us.

If you're unable to attend a session, we can figure out a time to reschedule, assuring you receive all your sessions for that month. If either of us will be out of town or unavailable, we can plan for that and adjust pricing, gym access (for training-only memberships), and anything else as needed.
